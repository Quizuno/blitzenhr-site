What BlitzenHR does

  • Builds the weekly schedule

    It reads each person's availability, approved time off and training, then follows the rules you set: how many people each shift needs, who can close, and everything else you'd otherwise have to keep track of by hand.

  • Keeps it fair, week after week

    Hard shifts, closes and hours are spread evenly over many weeks, not just this one. Nobody goes over 40 hours unless you put them there yourself.

  • Writes the hourly position sheet

    Who's on which station each hour of every shift. The sheet you used to fill in by hand before the rush is ready as soon as the schedule is.
